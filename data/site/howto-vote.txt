Voting History

Each week, players receive 10 points per tribe to assign among the Survivors on that tribe. Players earn Vote Points equal to the number of points they assigned to a Survivor who is voted out that week.

A player may assign no more than 10 points per Survivor. All votes reset at the start of each week. Votes must be submitted before the episode first airs on the US East coast. Submissions received after this time will not count. Voting reopens later that night after the episode scores have been posted.

A Survivor is considered “voted out” if any of the following occur:

They receive the most votes at tribal council;
They are eliminated after drawing rocks;
They lose a final fire-making challenge; or
They receive the most votes and go to another location such as Extinction Island.

In extraordinary circumstances, Fantasy Survivor reserves the right to determine, at its discretion, whether Vote Points will be awarded.

Players do not earn Vote Points for correctly selecting a Survivor who is medically evacuated or removed from the game by production.
