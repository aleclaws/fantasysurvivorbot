ABOUT FANTASY SURVIVOR
Earn points by correctly guessing the Survivor voted out each week, and when your drafted Survivors win challenges, find advantages and more. Compete against your friends to see who can outwit, outplay, outlast and score the most points.

VOTE WEEKLY

Guess the Survivor you think will be voted out at the next tribal council.
